A Decorative Lamp Made from a Recycled Computer Mouse

Reusing old materials is one way to reduce waste and protect the environment.
Electronic waste, including unused computer mice, can become a problem if it is not managed properly.
Instead of throwing away an old computer mouse, people can turn it into a decorative lamp.
This product combines creativity, technology, and environmental awareness.

A decorative lamp made from a computer mouse requires several materials.
The main material is an unused computer mouse.
Other materials include a low-voltage LED light, cables, a battery holder, cardboard for the base, and glue.
Some additional decorations can also be used to improve its appearance.
Before the production begins, the mouse and other materials are cleaned and prepared.

The construction begins by using the computer mouse as the main body of the lamp.
The LED light is arranged as the lighting source, while the cables and battery holder support its operation.
The cardboard is used as a base to keep the lamp stable.
After that, additional decorations are attached to make the product more beautiful.
The components are arranged properly to create a neat and unique design.

This decorative lamp has several advantages.
It turns an unused computer mouse into a useful decoration and gives the old material a new value.
The product can be displayed on a table or in a room.
Furthermore, it helps reduce electronic waste and promotes the reuse of materials.
Through this project, people can learn that creativity can turn waste into something useful and attractive.
