Turning an Old Computer Mouse into a Creative Lamp

Electronic waste is a problem that affects the environment. Many electronic devices, such as computer mice, are thrown away because they are broken or no longer used. However, some parts of these devices can still be reused. An old computer mouse can be transformed into a decorative lamp. This creative project gives a new purpose to an unused electronic device.

The process starts by collecting an old computer mouse and preparing the necessary materials. The materials include a low-voltage LED light, cables, a battery holder, cardboard, and glue. The mouse is cleaned before being used. < All materials are prepared carefully to make the production process easier and more organized.

After the preparation, the LED light is arranged inside the mouse as the main lighting component. The cables and battery holder are prepared to provide power to the LED. A cardboard base is added to support the lamp. The outside part of the mouse can also be decorated with additional ornaments. All components are arranged neatly to create an attractive decorative lamp.

The final product is a decorative lamp made from an old computer mouse. It can be placed on a study desk or used as a room decoration. Besides having decorative value, this product also helps reduce electronic waste. It encourages creativity and teaches people to reuse unused materials. This project proves that old electronic devices can still have value when they are used creatively.
