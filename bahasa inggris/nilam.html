How an Old Computer Mouse Becomes a Useful Decoration

Old electronic devices are often considered useless when they are damaged or no longer needed One example is a computer mouse that is no longer used. However, this item can be reused and transformed into a decorative lamp. This idea is part of a creative project called "Bekas Jadi Berkelas", which focuses on turning used materials into valuable products.

The decorative lamp is made from several simple materials. These materials include an old computer mouse, a low-voltage LED light, cables, a battery holder, cardboard, and glue. The computer mouse is used as the main part of the product. The LED provides light, while the cardboard acts as a supporting base. All materials are prepared before the making process begins.

First, the old computer mouse is cleaned and prepared. Then, the LED light and its supporting components are arranged carefully. The cardboard base is attached to help the lamp stand firmly. After the main components are arranged, additional decorations can be added to improve the appearance. The final arrangement creates a unique lamp from an unused computer mouse.

The result is a small decorative lamp that can be used to decorate a study desk or a room. It has an attractive appearance and provides additional lighting. This product also helps reduce electronic waste by reusing old materials. In addition, the project develops creativity, cooperation, and environmental awareness. Therefore, a used computer mouse can become a useful and valuable decorative product.
